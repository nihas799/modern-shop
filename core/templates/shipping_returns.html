Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about delivery, shipping charges, and returns.

Shipping Information
  • We ship across India within 2–5 business days.
  • Free shipping on orders above ₹999.
  • Orders below ₹999 may include a small shipping fee.
  • You will receive tracking details once your order is shipped.
Returns & Refunds
  • Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused and in original packaging.
  • Refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days.
  • COD refunds will be processed to your bank account.
Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, cancellation is not possible. You can cancel an order from the My Orders page.
